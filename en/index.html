A Leiden favourite since the ’80s

The owner at the time was a great admirer of the Italian composer Giuseppe Domenico Scarlatti and named his tea house after his musical favourite. What started as a tea house grew into a full restaurant.

We have proudly kept the name, because of our history and because it has a special meaning for many of our regular guests. We became known for our cosy restaurant, the sunny terrace, the varied menu, the atmospheric Heerenkamer and of course our enthusiastic staff.
