Good to know

  • We do not charge room hire, but there is a minimum spend: € 550 from Sunday to Thursday and € 700 on Friday and Saturday. If you do not reach it, we charge the difference as room hire.
  • Up to 22 people can sit at one long table. If you are with more, we set separate tables for up to 30.
  • Please let us know about dietary requirements or allergies in advance, so we can take them into account.
  • We prefer payment by debit card or cash. For an invoice we charge € 15 administration costs; you can leave your details here.
  • All our agreements are subject to the Uniform Conditions for the Hospitality Industry (UVH, in Dutch).
