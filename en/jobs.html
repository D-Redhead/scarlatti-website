Floor Manager (hours by agreement)

Are you a born leader with an eye for detail, a passion for hospitality and a heart for teamwork? Scarlatti is looking for a floor manager who is strong on the floor and thinks strategically behind the scenes.

What will you do?

As Floor Manager at Scarlatti you divide your time evenly between the floor and the office. Most of the time you are active on the floor to make sure everything runs smoothly and guests have a fantastic experience. The rest of the time you spend on office work, making sure the organisation behind the scenes runs just as well as on the floor.

Your main tasks

Restaurant management

  • Making sure everything runs like clockwork, from the guest experience to purchasing and sales
  • Coordinating group bookings: from request to visit (optional)
  • Responding quickly to what the situation requires
  • Spotting improvements and taking initiative
  • Ensuring correct administrative and financial handling

Staff management

  • Making schedules and arranging leave
  • Hiring new staff
  • Making sure new staff are properly onboarded
  • Holding performance reviews

Linking departments

  • Being the point of contact for HR, marketing and our other locations

Who are we looking for?

We are looking for a strong personality with organisational talent and a good sense of humour. You take responsibility and know how to motivate people. You also bring:

  • Experience in hospitality, preferably in a management role
  • Good communication skills
  • A practical attitude and an eye for detail
  • Affinity with both hospitality and management

What do we offer?

  • A challenging and varied position
  • Hours by agreement, a mix of operational and administrative tasks
  • Room for your own initiative and growth
  • A committed and friendly team
  • Bike lease scheme
  • Overtime can be paid out or taken as time off in lieu
  • Gross monthly salary between € 2,900 and € 3,300 based on full-time employment, depending on experience

Sounds like you?

Apply via the “Join our team” bar at the top of this page.

Dishwasher

As a dishwasher you make sure our kitchen keeps running smoothly. You keep the dishes, cutlery and kitchen equipment clean and make sure everything is ready for the team again on time.

You also help keep the kitchen clean and tidy, so our cooks can work in the best possible conditions. You are a doer who supports the team and contributes to a great experience for our guests.

Who we are looking for

  • You are hands-on and pitch in where needed
  • You work neatly and like a clean workplace
  • Work experience is nice but not required

What we offer you

  • Fixed working days (by agreement), both day and evening shifts
  • A market-rate salary
  • Opportunities for growth and development

Additional benefits

  • Tips
  • Holiday pay

Sounds like you?

Apply via the “Join our team” bar at the top of this page.
