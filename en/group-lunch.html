Groups

Group lunch

Lunch with a group in the Heerenkamer. If you are with 10 people or more, you choose one of the two lunch options below for the whole group.

Sandwich lunch

21.50 pp
  • Soup

    Choice of roasted bell pepper and tomato soup or our soup of the moment.

  • Sandwiches

    3 sandwiches per person with luxury fillings, with meat, fish and vegetarian. We serve them on platters, so everyone can take what they fancy.

Classic lunch

21.50 pp
  • Soup

    Choice of roasted bell pepper and tomato soup or our soup of the moment.

  • Lunch classic

    Served on brown or white rustic bread. Choice of:

    • Carpaccio with Italian cheese, truffle mayonnaise, pine nuts and arugula
    • Avocado toast with avocado, boiled egg, pine nuts and sun-dried tomatoes
    • Fried eggs (cheese/ham/tomato/all)
    • Oma Bob’s beef or wild mushroom croquettes
    • Fish trio with smoked salmon, tuna salad and 2 gamba croquettes

Choosing the classic lunch? Let us know at least 5 working days in advance who is having which soup and which dish. After that we charge € 2.50 pp extra.

Fancy something sweet afterwards? For € 4 pp extra you make it a three-course lunch, with cheesecake or apple pie to finish (whipped cream +€ 0.75).

Good to know

  • Please let us know about dietary requirements or allergies in advance, so we can take them into account.
  • Room hire, minimum spend, table layout and payment: all agreements for the Heerenkamer.
