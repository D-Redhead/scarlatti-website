High tea & high wine

Catching up with your best friend, a baby shower or simply enjoying an afternoon out? Join us for a high tea or high wine full of homemade treats.

Book a table

We serve our highs on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, for 2 to 30 people.

High tea

24.50 pp

Unlimited tea and a tiered stand with savoury and sweet bites

  • Bell pepper and tomato soup
  • Toast with smoked salmon and crème fraîche
  • Sandwich with young cheese, mustard and mesclun salad
  • Wrap with guacamole, ribeye and sun-dried tomato
  • Scones with strawberry jam
  • Brownie
  • Cheesecake
  • Macaron
  • Petit four

Our high tea is also available vegetarian!

High wine

32.50 pp

3 glasses of wine, served in three rounds

Round 1

  • Oven-baked bread
  • Italian ham
  • Cubes of aged cheese
  • Smoked salmon rolls with crème fraîche and chives

Round 2

  • Fried beef strips with mushroom
  • Salad of the moment
  • Bell pepper and tomato soup
  • Nachos

Round 3

  • Grand dessert (homemade sweets)

Our high wine is also available vegetarian!

Coming with a larger group?

Then you can book the Heerenkamer exclusively. There is room for up to 22 people at one long table, and for 30 at separate tables.
