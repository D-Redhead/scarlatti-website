Groups

Drinks in the Heerenkamer

Drinks of your choice and bites from our kitchen. Below you can read everything that is possible and what it costs. We are happy to discuss your wishes in advance, so the evening runs smoothly.

Drinks

There are two options for drinks: you pay for what has been drunk, or you choose a drinks package with a fixed price per person. Want to start your party extra festively? We can serve a glass of prosecco (€ 6) or a cocktail (€ 8.50) on arrival.

Pay for what has been drunk

We keep track of what is drunk and settle based on what has actually been consumed. A few prices to give you an idea:

  • Lager (small glass)3.95
  • Soft drink3.60
  • Bottle of winefrom 33.00

Your own beer tap

We can put a tap in the Heerenkamer, so you can pour your own beer. A 20-litre keg costs € 270 and is good for about 80 small glasses. That saves you almost € 0.60 per glass. We only charge for the keg if it has been tapped.

Wine and water on the table

On request we put bottles of wine and water on the table. You only pay for the bottles that are opened.

Would you like to taste the wines first or discuss other options? Feel free to drop by.

Drinks package

Three hours of drinks from the bar, served by our team.

  • Basic29.50 pp

    lager, wine, soft drinks, coffee and tea · each extra hour € 10 pp

  • Extended36.50 pp

    lager, specialty beer, wine, soft drinks, coffee and tea · each extra hour € 12.50 pp

Prefer to buy off all drinks? That is possible on request.

Excluding drinks

Would you like certain drinks not to be served, such as specialty beer or cocktails? Let us know in advance.

Bites

With your group you choose one of the two packages.

Scarlatti bites package

6.00 pp
  • On the table: nuts
  • 3 rounds of a mix of our luxury fried bites

Luxury bites package

9.00 pp
  • On the table: nuts and nachos with salsa and guacamole
  • Round 1: lightly peppered fuet from Catalonia
  • Round 2: truffle fries with truffle mayonnaise and grana padano
  • Round 3: a mix of our luxury fried bites
  • Vegetarian option available

A presentation or slideshow? You can use our projector for € 25.

Good to know

  • Let us know at least 5 working days in advance how you would like to arrange the drinks, which bites package you choose and how many people are coming. After that we charge for the last number given.
  • The Heerenkamer must be empty by 00:00 from Sunday to Thursday and by 01:00 on Friday and Saturday.
  • Decorating is allowed, but we prefer to keep confetti and glitter out.
  • For a standing drinks party you can come with up to 70 people.
  • Room hire, minimum spend, table layout and payment: all agreements for the Heerenkamer.
