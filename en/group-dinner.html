Groups

Group dinner

Dinner with a group in the Heerenkamer. If you are with 10 people or more, we serve our three-course set menu with choices. This menu costs € 41.50 pp.

Would you like bread with sea salt butter and extra virgin olive oil on the table on arrival? No problem! We charge € 2.50 pp for this.

Starter

Choose one of three

  • Carpaccio

    Italian cheese · truffle mayonnaise · pine nuts · arugula

  • Gambas à la Ricard

    pan-fried gambas · Ricard pastis · garlic · tender leek · bread

  • Pumpkin flammkuchen (v)

    pumpkin purée · roasted cauliflower · pomegranate · spicy mayonnaise

Main course

We serve fries with mayonnaise with the main course

  • Pieterman (weever fillet)

    pan-seared · pea purée · black salsify · green herbs

  • Scarlatti’s steak

    hanger steak · parsnip purée · roasted pumpkin · morel gravy

  • Celeriac steak (v)

    crispy polenta · miso · daikon radish · romesco

Dessert

Prefer a cheese board instead of dessert? For € 3 pp extra we will gladly arrange it.

  • Grand Dessert

    three homemade sweets

Good to know

  • Would you like to change something on the menu? Let us know and we will gladly think along. We also take dietary requirements and allergies into account if you let us know in advance.
  • Let us know at least 5 working days in advance who is choosing what and how many people are coming. After that we charge € 2.50 pp extra for the menu choice.
  • Room hire, minimum spend, table layout and payment: all agreements for the Heerenkamer.
